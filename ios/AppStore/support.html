Practice blackjack basic strategy, card counting, true count, and Hi-Lo deviations - instantly graded, with no wagering.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap Continue to start the last trainer, or pick another from the home screen.
  • Answer each hand; correct answers advance automatically, and a miss pauses to show you the correct play and why.
  • Set your daily goal, table rules, counting system, and drill speed in Settings.

Frequently asked

Is this real gambling?

No. Blackjack Trainer is an educational strategy trainer. There is no wagering and no real or virtual money - it only tracks how accurately you play.

Where are my stats stored? Are they private?

On your device, and - if you turn on iCloud sync - in your own iCloud account. Nothing is collected by the developer. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I sync between my iPhone and other devices?

Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device. Stats sync automatically through iCloud.

What do the counting systems mean?

The app supports Hi-Lo plus many other balanced and unbalanced systems. Each system's card values are shown before a drill begins.
