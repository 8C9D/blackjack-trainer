Blackjack Trainer

Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Blackjack Trainer is an educational blackjack strategy trainer. The short version of this policy: the app collects nothing about you.

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No permissions. Your practice stats stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Blackjack Trainer has no user accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs, and it requests no system permissions. The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any information about you or how you use the app.

What the app stores, and where

Your practice statistics and preferences (accuracy, streaks, daily goal, table rules, and counting settings) are saved only on your device using the system's standard on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

If a future update enables iCloud sync, those same statistics would sync only through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store), never through the developer or any third party. This version of the app does not sync.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other apps or websites and contains no tracking technologies.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
